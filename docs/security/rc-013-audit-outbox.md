# RC-013 — transactional audit outbox

`security_audit_events` is the authoritative, append-only audit ledger. Its
required semantic fields are event type, severity, action and outcome; tenant,
branch, actor, request, target and sanitized state/metadata are persisted when
available. Database triggers reject updates and deletes.

## Caller inventory and scope

- Guest order creation and cancellation were critical mutations recorded with
  fire-and-forget calls after commit. They now enqueue inside their existing
  business transaction, keyed by tenant plus operation and the committed order
  ID.
- Order state transitions already insert `security_audit_events` in their
  business transaction and remain unchanged.
- Authentication denials, authorization/rate-limit denials, session activity,
  payment observations and the HTTP interceptor await or invoke `record` as
  operational observations. `recordBestEffort` makes the interceptor contract
  explicit; these events are not the evidence for committing a business
  mutation and remain outside this narrowly scoped RC.
- `AuditService.record` previously caught every persistence error, logged only
  its sanitized Prisma code and returned `null`. Critical storefront paths no
  longer use that contract.

Request IDs are generated by the server bootstrap. Outbox operation identity
does not rely on them: `(tenant_id, operation_id)` is unique and the operation
contains the server-created order ID. A canonical payload hash rejects reuse of
that identity with divergent content. The worker persists all tenant, branch,
actor and target context and never depends on request-local storage.

## Delivery and crash boundaries

Each business commit includes its outbox row or rolls back. Workers on every
replica atomically claim bounded batches with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, a claim
token and expiring lease. Insert into `security_audit_events` and the fenced
`DELIVERED` transition share one transaction. The unique nullable
`outbox_event_id` leaves history untouched and prevents a second authoritative
event.

Consequently crashes before business commit leave neither record; crashes after
it leave durable pending work; crashes around claim or delivery are recovered
after lease expiry; transaction failures leave neither partial delivery; and a
stale attempt cannot finalize a newer claim. A committed delivery is safely
recognized through the unique outbox link. Retry uses bounded exponential
backoff and ends in durable `FAILED_FINAL` state for reconciliation.

Shutdown hooks stop new claims and wait only for a configured bounded period.
They are an optimization, not the durability mechanism: unfinished leases stay
in PostgreSQL and can be reclaimed by another replica.
